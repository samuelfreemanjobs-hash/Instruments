# Memphis producer archetypes → kit use (original only)

**Product lane:** 90s Memphis rap / phonk drum kit — not East Coast boom-bap, not modern trap roll packs.

These are **internal ear targets** for arranging CNV masters. Do **not** put artist names in storefront filenames or marketing titles. Do **not** claim these loops recreate any tape.

Research basis: Loaded Samples Memphis production guide + public interviews `[inspected]` — gear associations are historical context for *technique*, not endorsement.

| Internal lane | Historical gear cue | How they’d dig into this pack | Banks / folders |
|---------------|---------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------|
| **Converter Crawl** | DR-660 / early underground (Zirk-era energy) | Build whole beats from pitched long 808s + cowbell “melodies”; almost no sample loops; slow crawl 90–98 | `02/Pitched_Sub_Basses`, `01/Percussion` cowbells, `cnv-dr660-*` / `cnv-arch-crawl-*` loops |
| **Bitcrush Bed** | SP-1200 + DR-660 (Squeeky-era energy) | Chop dirty kicks/snares; pitch-shift one-shots until alias grit sings; keep patterns simple but *heavy* | `02/Aliased_One_Shots`, BIT12 kicks/snares, `04/` dust under the kit |
| **Horror Snap** | SP-1200 / W-30 / MPC grit (Paul-era energy) | Dark roomy claps + hollow snares; sinister sparse hats; layer crash/FX as stabs; leave space for horror keys elsewhere | `01/Snares` CAVE, `01/Crashes_Rides`, `04/` hum/noise, thin tops |
| **Club Bounce** | Three 6 club / Juicy-era bounce | Same Memphis grid but thicker kick/snare punch, slightly busier hats, still half-time — party menace not trap rolls | Punchy `01/Kicks`+`Snares`, EDGE closed hats, RATE sub under the bounce |

## Arrangement rules (all lanes)

1. Prefer **94** tape-creep or **134** as half-time push — not 140 trap default.
2. **Drums-first:** pitched subs/cowbells can carry melody; don’t require piano beds in the pack.
3. Short decays on tops; long decays on RATE beds; choke so notes don’t smear.
4. Cassette dust (`04/`) is a *layer*, not the whole drum.
