================================================================================

All shippable WAVs in this pack are original synthesis produced with the
DiskLordz DRUMSMITH workflow (recipe-driven DSP). They are inspired by
vintage converter / tape / rompler aesthetics associated with Memphis and
phonk production — they are NOT brand-machine ROM dumps and NOT cleared
third-party sample packs.

included in the commercial download.
