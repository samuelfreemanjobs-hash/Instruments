# Research: Top ~20 1990s Memphis rap figures → style, archetype, groove, FX

**Question:** Who are the most historically cited early/mid‑1990s Memphis rap artists/producers, and what styles, groove habits, and FX practices should inform Converter Night (without cloning tapes)?  
**Success:** Named roster with sources; archetype → groove/FX table tagged; phonk anachronism called out.  
**Non-goals:** Ranking by streams; shipping artist-named SKUs; claiming exact MIDI of any release; implementing new DSP this turn.

**Verdict:** Treat “phonk” as a **2010s revival label** sampling 1990s Memphis aesthetics — not a 1990 contemporary genre name `[inspected]` (Wikipedia *Phonk*). The useful “top 20” is a **historically cited underground + Hypnotize orbit set**, not a 1990 chart. Drum language clusters into four kit archetypes already in `recipes/MEMPHIS_ARCHETYPES.md` (crawl / bitcrush / horror / bounce). Weakest tag on the verdict: **[inspected]** (secondary journalism + vendor guide); BPM/MIDI grids below that are **[assumed]** technique translations.

### Conflict / dissent

- **Vendor guide vs journalism:** Loaded Samples lists many DR-660 users and detailed techniques; it is a sample-seller’s blog (`[inspected]`, secondary). RBMA + Memphis Flyer are stronger on scene narrative; gear specifics still often rest on interviews + community lore. Prefer Hall of Fame / Flyer / RBMA for *who mattered*; use LS for *technique vocabulary* with caution.  
- **Paul vs Squeeky origin story:** Squeeky claims early Triple Six cribbed his mixtape sound `[inspected]` Flyer/RBMA — historical beef, not settled “who invented Memphis.” For kits: credit **both** Squeeky (rat-a-tat SP bed) and Paul (horror/sample theater) as separate archetypes.

## Archetype → MIDI groove & FX (kit-facing)

| Archetype | BPM intent | Groove (MIDI sketch) | FX / process |
|-----------|------------|----------------------|--------------|
| **Converter Crawl** | ~90–98 (CNV uses **94**) | Kick sparse; snare on 3 (half-time); hats quarter/8th thin; **pitched 808** as bassline with choke/retrigger; **cowbell/clave** max-decay as melody | Little send FX; cassette dust as *layer*; long sub decays `[inspected]` LS + `[assumed]` CNV |
| **Bitcrush Bed** | ~90–100 or half-time ~130–140 | Heavier kick/snare; “ts-ts-ts” closed hats; few melodic drums; alias/pitch-shift grit on one-shots | SP-1200 character = bitcrush/alias + vinyl chop dirt `[inspected]` Flyer/LS |
| **Horror Snap** | ~85–95 or half-time | Room for double-time MCs; hollow snare/clap; crash/stab hits as punctuation; leave midrange for scary keys | Vocal flanger/reverb rare but signature when present; sample stabs; mixer FX `[inspected]` LS |
| **Club Bounce** | ~130–140 as half-time (~65–70 feel) or mid 90s bounce | Punchier K/S; slightly busier hats; still **not** modern trap rolls | Cleaner tops; RATE sub under party `[assumed]` + HoF commercial era |

**CNV alignment `[inspected]`:** crawl → `cnv-dr660-*` / `cnv-arch-crawl`; bitcrush → `cnv-arch-bitcrush`; horror → `cnv-arch-horror`; bounce → `cnv-arch-bounce` @ 134.
