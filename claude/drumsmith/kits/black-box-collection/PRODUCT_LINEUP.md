## Releases

### BLACK BOX 93 — Memphis Archives
**The Underground Era**  
Dark, minimal, haunted Memphis rap percussion.  
Signature: hollow 808 kicks, papery snares, dry claps, eerie cowbells, tape-degraded hats, grimy percussion.  
**Target: 175–225 samples** · SKU `BB93`

### BLACK BOX 96 — The Original Machine  ★ FLAGSHIP
**The Signature Era**  
Vintage Memphis drums + dirty hardware character + hard-hitting 90s Southern rhythms.  
Signature: thick 808s, aggressive snares, metallic cowbells, crunchy hats, analog toms, dry claps.  
**Target: 200–250 samples** · SKU `BB96`

### BLACK BOX 98 — Trunk Pressure
**The Bass Era**  
Late-90s underground street trunk-rattlers.  
Signature: heavy sub kicks, booming 808 bass, cracking snares, layered claps, rimshots, hard percussion.  
**Target: 175–225 samples** · SKU `BB98`

### BLACK BOX 2000 — Southern Circuitry
**The Digital Transition**  
Dusty underground → crisp early-2000s Southern.  
Signature: punchy digital kicks, snappy snares, bright claps, tight hats, synthesized percussion, tuned 808 bass.  
**Target: 200–250 samples** · SKU `BB00`

### DIRTY CIRCUITS — Corrupted Machine
Distorted, crushed, saturated drums (bus/process lane across eras).

### BLACK BOX 96: Dirty Circuits Edition — Expanded Flagship
