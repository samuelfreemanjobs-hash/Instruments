# Night Circuit — preset category taxonomy

Each category is a **role in a beat**, not a marketing tag. Factory presets stay **100% synthetic** (oscillators only).

| Category | Job in the mix | Play style | Envelope bias | Not this |
|----------|----------------|------------|---------------|----------|
| **Bass** | Sub and low-end weight | Single notes, slides | Long sustain, slow filter | Chord stacks |
| **Synth** | **Playable poly — chords & melodic beds** | **Voicings, progressions, vaporwave/Jeezy stacks** | **Medium attack, solid sustain, stable filter** | Solo leads, one-shot plucks |
| **Lead** | Top-line / hook mono focus | Single-note runs | Often faster attack than Synth | Wide chord voicings |
| **Pad** | Atmosphere and space | Long holds, background | Slow amp/filter, high sustain | Tight rhythmic chords |
| **Pluck** | Rhythmic stabs | Short, percussive | Near-zero sustain | Pad or chord body |
| **Keys** | Staccato keyboard cues | Short–medium, keyed riffs | Decay-forward | Lush poly stacks |
| **Synth FX** | Sweeps, sirens, transitions | Effect gestures | Extreme filter env | Musical harmony parts |

## Synth vs Lead vs Pad (quick A&R check)

- **Synth:** sounds good with **3+ notes** held; filter stays musically open under chords.
- **Lead:** sounds best **monophonic** or unison; may feel thin or harsh on full triads.
- **Pad:** fills space; not meant as the main **played** chord instrument in the beat.

## Night Circuit (flagship Synth preset)

Default program on load. Target: warm detuned stack, moderate filter, enough sustain for trap/soul chord voicings and vaporwave-style extensions.
