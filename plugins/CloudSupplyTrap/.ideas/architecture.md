# CloudSupplyTrap — architecture

**Genre:** Trap, phonk, ethereal bells.

**Layer A:** 3-op FM glass/mallet + sine/tri 808 sub w/ pitch-drop punch.  
**Layer B:** 5-vowel formant morph + breath air + 12-frame crystal WT.  
**Filter:** 24dB LPF/BP/HP.  
**FX:** Ping-pong delay, octave shimmer hall reverb, tube bite.  
**Macros:** Tone, Bell/FM, Formant, Sub Punch, Dirt, Flutter, Ping-Pong, Shimmer.

**UI:** Obsidian, electric cyan, violet. XY Y-axis → cutoff + shimmer.

**Presets (×12):** Cloud Nine Bells, Dark Memphis Choir, Ethereal Trap Flute, Sub & Crystal Keys, Haunted Phonk Pluck, Midnight OVO Pad, Glass Mallet Drop, Distorted Bell Lead, Velvet Vocal Swell, 808 Sub Atmos, Frozen Harp, Astral Drift.
