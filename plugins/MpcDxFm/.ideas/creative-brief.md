# MpcDxFm — creative brief

**Target:** Yamaha **DX7 / TX81Z**-class **FM** for **MPC Sample / Melodee**: **4- or 6-operator** sine FM, **lightweight math** (no heavy analog filter modeling), **high polyphony** on ARM (initial goal **16 voices** @ 4-op, **8 voices** @ 6-op).

**Sonic goals:** electric piano, metallic bells, slap bass, bright G-funk leads (West Coast).

**v1 scope:** **4-op** engine with **TX81Z-style** multi-sine waveforms optional on carrier/modulators (simplified set: sine, half-sine, alt sine); **8 canonical algorithms** (not full DX32). **6-op** mode and full 32 DX algorithms = v2.

**Not** a ROMpler or SysEx bulk-dump clone — new patch format in APVTS + factory programs.

**Host:** headless; **K1–K3** = **output level / brightness (op2 level)**, **feedback**, **algorithm** (or detune macro).
