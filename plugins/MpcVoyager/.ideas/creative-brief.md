# MpcVoyager — creative brief

**Minimoog Voyager**-style **mono** synth for Melodee: **3 morph oscillators** (tri/saw/pulse), **sub on osc3**, **LPF24 ladder** + drive, dual **filter/amp ADSR**, **glide**, noise. Headless; K1=cutoff, K2=res, K3=filter env amount.

**v1:** single ladder (dual serial ladders = v2). **Not** a Moog trademark clone — “Voyager-inspired” DSP.

**Programs:** West Coast / G-funk, DJ Battlecat-style bass, sub slides, talkbox-adjacent leads (factory table; expandable preset bank).
