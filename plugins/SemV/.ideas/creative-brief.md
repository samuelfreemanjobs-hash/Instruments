# SemV — creative brief

**Pick (1 of Arturia V Collection):** [**SEM V**](https://www.arturia.com/products/software-synths/sem-v/overview) — Oberheim SEM timbre with Arturia’s arp/FX/macro polish.

**Why this SKU (not Jun-6 / Mini / Jup-8):** Repo already covers **Juno** (`MpcJuno106`) and **Minimoog** (`MpcMinimoog`) on the Melodee lane. **SEM** gives a **12 dB state-variable** voice (LP/BP/HP/notch) and **dual-osc pulse/saw** character without duplicating those MPC classics. Scope stays **L2** for a credible v1 clone, not full ArturiaFX suite parity.

**Product:** `SemV` — polyphonic **virtual analog** VST3 + Standalone for **Windows/macOS/Linux desktop** (JUCE native UI). Optional future **MpcSemV** headless port is out of scope for this APC track.

**Sonic goal:** Warm **East Coast** leads and pads — resonant **notch/brass** sweeps, **PWM strings**, **mono legato** bass. Reference: Oberheim SEM + Arturia’s **chorus + delay + plate** insert chain (simplified v1).

**Non-goals v1:** Full **8 SEM stack** browser, MPE, user wavetable import, Arturia **FX Collection** depth, or 1:1 GUI skin.

**Audience:** Producers wanting SEM color in the DAW without Kontakt/Arturia host; factory preset bank for instant “SEM brass / PWM pad / acid notch”.

**Visual:** Compact **Arturia-inspired** dark panel — gold/orange accents on charcoal; primary focus on filter mode + dual osc level, not Play-Series WebView.
