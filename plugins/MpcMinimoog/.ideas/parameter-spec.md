# MpcMinimoog — parameters (v1)

Melodee **K1–K3** = IDs 1–3.

| # | ID | Name | Range | Default |
|---|-----|------|-------|---------|
| 1 | `cutoff` | Filter cutoff | 20 Hz–20 kHz (skew) | 400 |
| 2 | `emphasis` | Emphasis (res) | 0–1 | 0.25 |
| 3 | `glide` | Glide time | 0–3 s | 0.08 |
| 4 | `osc1_wave` | Osc 1 | 0=tri 1=saw 2=square | 1 |
| 5 | `osc2_wave` | Osc 2 | 0–2 | 1 |
| 6 | `osc3_wave` | Osc 3 | 0–2 | 0 |
| 7 | `osc1_level` | Osc 1 level | 0–1 | 1 |
| 8 | `osc2_level` | Osc 2 level | 0–1 | 0.6 |
| 9 | `osc3_level` | Osc 3 level | 0–1 | 0.4 |
| 10 | `osc2_detune` | Osc 2 detune | ±12 sem | 0 |
| 11 | `osc3_detune` | Osc 3 detune | ±12 sem | -7 |
| 12 | `noise_level` | Noise | 0–1 | 0 |
| 13 | `filter_eg_amt` | Filter contour | 0–1 | 0.5 |
| 14 | `drive` | Ladder drive | 0–1 | 0.15 |
| 15 | `attack` | Amp attack | 1 ms–5 s | 5 ms |
| 16 | `decay` | Decay | 1 ms–8 s | 200 ms |
| 17 | `sustain` | Sustain | 0–1 | 0.85 |
| 18 | `release` | Release | 1 ms–8 s | 300 ms |
| 19 | `drift` | Oscillator drift | 0–1 | 0.2 |

**Programs:** Init Bass, Cream Lead, Funk Sub, Noise Stab, Glide Solo.
