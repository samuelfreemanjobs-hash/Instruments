# AnalogDreams84 — architecture

**Genre:** 80s synthwave / R&B brass / warm pads.

**Layer A:** Dual PWM/saw PolyBLEP, sub, thermal pitch drift.  
**Layer B:** 12-frame 80s digital WT + FM bell brass/choir.  
**FX:** Triple-mode BBD chorus, gated 80s plate reverb.  
**Macros:** Cutoff, Drift, PWM/Shape, Warmth, Swell, BBD Chorus, Echo, 80s Plate.

**UI:** Neon magenta, midnight blue, cyan. XY pad + Dice + 8 macros.

**Presets (×12):** Miami Sunset Brass, Blade Runner Strings, Neon Arp Pluck, 84 Tape Polysynth, Velvet Sub Pad, Crystal FM Keys, Midnight Drive Lead, Dreamy BBD Choir, Analog Bassline, VHS Nostalgia, Jupiter Swell, Starlight Bells.
