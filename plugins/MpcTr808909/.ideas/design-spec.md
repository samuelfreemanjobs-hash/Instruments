# MpcTr808909 — design (v1 headless)

**Melodee K1–K3:** `focus_decay`, `focus_tune`, `drive` (first three APVTS parameters).

**MIDI:** GM kit — C1 kick (36), D1 snare (38), F#1 hat (42). Mono retrigger per voice.

**DSP:** Kick = swept sine amp env; snare = band tone + noise env; hat = short noise + square click.

**Programs:** 808 Classic, 909 House, Hybrid Punch, Kick Sub.
