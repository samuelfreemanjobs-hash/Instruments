# PlaySynthDreams — creative brief

**Product:** 12-voice **pure synth** (no WAV streaming) inspired by **Native Instruments Kontakt Play Series** — *Analog Dreams*, *Cloud Supply*, *Lo-Fi Glow*.

**Hook:** Every voice runs **Layer A** (warm analog/keys) and **Layer B** (digital air/choir textures) in parallel, mixed with an **equal-power A/B blend** and **8 producer macros** that retarget many DSP params at once.

**Audience:** Producers who want mix-ready lo-fi / retro / ambient tones without sample libraries.

**Variants (same engine, different skin + default macros + preset pack):**

| SKU | Accent | Preset bias |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| PlaySynthDreams | Obsidian / violet / cyan (umbrella) | Full 12-preset pack |
| PlaySynthAnalogDreams | Warm amber / cream | Brass, keys, 40s electric |
| PlaySynthCloudSupply | Ice blue / white | Bells, choir, mallet |
| PlaySynthLoFiGlow | Dusty rose / tape brown | Tape pad, VHS strings, cassette rhodes |
