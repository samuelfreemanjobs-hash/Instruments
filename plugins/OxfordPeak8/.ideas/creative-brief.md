# OxfordPeak8 — creative brief

**Product:** 8-voice hybrid VST3 inspired by **Novation Peak** — three “New Oxford” digital oscillators per voice, analog-style multimode filter, **three saturation stages**, and a **16-slot dual-source mod matrix**.

**Sound goals:** Crisp wavetable/FM harmonics, warm pre-filter drive, resonant filter bite, post-filter grit, glued poly chords via master OD, lush chorus / delay / reverb.

**UI:** Dark charcoal, anodized blue, amber LED accents; tabs for Osc (×3), Filter, Envelopes, LFOs, Mod Matrix (16 rows), FX.

**Scope v1:** 8-voice poly; 16-frame wavetable bank per osc (expandable); white noise (pink optional v1.1). Not a hardware ROM dump — original wavetables only.
