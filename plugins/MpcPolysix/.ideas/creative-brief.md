# MpcPolysix — creative brief

**Target:** Korg **Polysix**: single **VCO** + **sub**, **SSM2044-style** resonant filter (12/24 dB character), **ensemble chorus** and lightweight **phaser** in FX section. Warm 6-voice pads; CPU-conscious ensemble (modulated delays, not full 3-tap BBD).

**Melodee:** K1–K3 = cutoff, resonance, ensemble mix.
