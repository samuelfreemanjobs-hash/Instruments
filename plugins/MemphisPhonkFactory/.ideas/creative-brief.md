# Memphis Phonk Factory — creative brief

## Product

Standalone + VST3 **Memphis phonk drum kit and loop factory**: procedural 808-inspired one-shots (kick, snare, hats, cowbell) and dusty 2-bar breaks with MPC swing, cassette saturation, 12-bit crush, and tape wow/flutter — matching the SP-1200 / 4-track Memphis aesthetic.

## User

Beatmakers who want fresh MPC-ready WAV folders without hunting samples: 20 one-shot variations + 3 tempo-matched loops.

## Sonic goals

- Punchy pitch-swept 808 kicks with click transients
- Papery snares (tonal body + band-limited noise)
- TR-808-style metallic hats
- Detuned square cowbell
- Loops at 112 / 118 / 124 BPM with 16th hats, classic kick/snare grid, subtle swing

## Out of scope (v1)

- Custom pattern editor (fixed Memphis grids only)
- Multi-output stems per drum voice
