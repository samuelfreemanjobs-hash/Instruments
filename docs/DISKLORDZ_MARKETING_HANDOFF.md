## Elevator pitch

Disklordz is an **underground phonk product line**: curated drum kits (**Memphis Vault**), a **web factory** that generates kits from prompts, and a **launch funnel** (free Memphis vocal chops → tape/plugin tripwires). Producers get **results in advance** (browser Cassette Smash demo) before email capture.
