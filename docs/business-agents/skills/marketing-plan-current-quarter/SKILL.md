## Quarter at a glance

**Theme:** **Ship clear stories for each product lane** — WAVE-909 as the JUCE factory hero, Disklordz SaaS as kit workflow (not the 808 PWA), and zero conflation between Junova-X / NovaDrum / TR-808 PWA.

**Top 3 objectives:**

1. **WAVE-909 awareness among trap/phonk producers** — grow qualified waitlist/demo interest with spec-accurate copy (no fake “RELEASE READY” marketing).
2. **Disklordz SaaS comprehension** — prospects understand “generate kit → preview → export WAV” vs browser 808 PWA vs plugins.
3. **Product-matrix hygiene in all touchpoints** — every external asset passes compliance on lane integrity; ship-eval on customer-facing bundles.

**Total marketing budget:** **$12,000** (paid + tools; excludes owner time)  
**Agent spend authorization cap (single commit):** **$25,000** — escalate above per brand memory  
**Headcount:** Owner + agent teammates (no dedicated FTE marketing org)  
**Quarter dates:** 2026-07-01 – 2026-09-30 *(refresh Q4 plan by 2026-10-07)*

- **Why now:** WAVE-909 (track **G**) is factory reference; messaging must land before wider beta outreach.
- **Budget allocated:** $5,000 (~42%)

- **Success metric:** Waitlist/demo signups (owner-tracked); **no fabricated counts in copy**

### Priority 2: Disklordz kit buyers (SaaS)

- **Who:** Producers who buy or generate drum kits online; MPC Sample exporters.
- **Why now:** SaaS v0 narrative must stay separate from TR-808 PWA.
- **Budget allocated:** $4,000 (~33%)
- **Owner:** Owner

- **Success metric:** Signups + first kit generation (owner dashboard); cite only with substantiation

### Priority 3: Mobile / browser drum users (TR-808 PWA)

- **Who:** Phone-first producers; MPC Sample users needing quick 808 patterns.
- **Why now:** Track **P** is independent; install/export story is the hook.
- **Budget allocated:** $1,500 (~12%)
