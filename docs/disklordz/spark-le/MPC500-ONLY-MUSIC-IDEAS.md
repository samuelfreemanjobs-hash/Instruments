## Performance ideas (no PC running)

### Chop & sample

1. **Loop sermon** — Load `Loops/Tops_Kickless_2bar.wav` from any pack; **Note Variation** / chop by bar; keep only 3 hits per bar in the sequence.  
2. **Bank double** — Bank A = session 1 one-shots; Bank B = session 2 same pad roles; **mute tracks** to drop from gangsta to screw without changing BPM.  
3. **Resample menace** — Record 4 bars seq A → new sample → transpose down + filter in MPC → new pad 1 for a “pre-screw” layer.  
4. **16-level hat** — Pad 5 only: velocity layers from brush (3) vs closed (1/2) for jazz rap ghosts without extra samples.

### Sequencer & arrangement

5. **48-track prayer** — Unmute **one track per pass**: pass 1 = pad 1 only; pass 2 = + pad 2; … until full kit — avoids mud early.  
6. **One-bar air** — Bar 8 of every 8: **all pads muted** except pad 7 rim once — street pause (gangsta), not EDM drop silence.  
7. **Trunk grid vs cruise grid** — Same pads, two sequences: seq 1 trap kick (1 + 3); seq 3 jazz kick (**1 only**, softer velocity).  
8. **Pad 8 as turn** — Cowbell/FX hit **once per 4 bars** on phonk/gangsta; **once per 8 bars** on smooth jazz (session 3 rule).

### Mix & pocket

9. **Knock-first mix** — Set pad 2 ** louder than pad 1** in the mix screen for session 1 only; flip for session 3 (sub under rim/hats).  
10. **Hat mute breakdown** — Bars 9–12: pads 5–6 muted, pads 1–4 + 7 only — vocal pocket prep.  
11. **Pressure then scripture** — Live: play seq A 140 live, stop, switch kit to B @ 72 **without** stopping recorder — one take “suite.”
