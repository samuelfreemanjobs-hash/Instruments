# DL001 — Boulevard 86 (French touch / filter disco)

## Lane archaeology

Boulevard 86 treats **1977–1983 disco-funk, boogie, and filter-house sample behavior** as source clay, re-forged into **Paris retro-futurist French touch** — not Southern phonk. The downstream story is **late-1990s/filter-house production**: sidechain pump, talkbox/vocoder, **909/707** grids, **118–124 BPM** for deep work and night-cruise programming.
