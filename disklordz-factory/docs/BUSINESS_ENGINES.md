# Three business engines

```text
             DISKLORDZ RECORDS
                    │
             creates music
                    ↓
             DISKLORDZ SUPPLY
                    │
            creates products
                    ↓
              DISKLORDZ LABS
                    │
             creates software
                    ↓
             attracts producers
                    ↓
             DISKLORDZ RECORDS
```

| Engine | Channels | Factory outputs |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| **Records** | YouTube, Spotify, Apple, TikTok, … | Tracks, mixes, Shorts, metadata |
| **Supply** | DiskLordz Store, marketplaces | Kits, 808 packs, presets, MIDI, bundles |
| **Labs** | Plugins, subscriptions | Instruments/FX; catalog as plugin content |

**Flywheel:** market data → music → catalog → content + products → audience + customers → analytics → better batches.

**Economics:** every asset gets a scorecard (generation cost vs YouTube, streaming, product, licensing revenue) to drive batch decisions, not vanity metrics.
