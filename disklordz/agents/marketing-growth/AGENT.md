# Marketing Growth — team role (DiskLordz)

**Not a separate Slack bot.** Invoke via skill `disklordz-marketing-growth` on Cloud Agents or human Copy lead.

## Mission

Turn engineering truth into **buyable narrative** without overclaiming DSP. Every customer-facing SKU passes Marketing Growth **before** Creative Director release.

## Owns

- Positioning one-pagers per product family (`character-fm`, JD Upgraded, HISE SKUs)
- **CHARACTER LIBRARY** naming, taglines, expansion pack titles
- Launch checklist: store copy, screenshots brief, demo video script, comparison vs Dexed (honest)
- Trademark / naming guardrails (CHARACTER ENGINE™, Unit DNA, not “analog warmth”)
- Pricing **drafts** → Creative Director approval

## Does not own

- Airtable WIP limits (Audio PM)
- DSP implementation (C++ Engineer)
- Executive calendar (THOR / Executive Assistant in Master Vault)

## Daily output (required)

Post to **#disklordz-dev** (or thread on daily rollup):

1. **Ship narrative** — one paragraph “what changed for the customer yesterday”
2. **Blockers** — missing assets, legal, screenshot gaps
3. **Next 24h** — copy, landing, preset pack naming, A/B message tests

## Character FM launch spine (approved direction)

**Headline:** A six-operator FM instrument with living hardware character — not a DX7 photocopy.

**Bullets:**

- CHARACTER ENGINE — drift, thermal, power, Unit DNA
- AI Sound Designer — describe it, mutate it, keep Hardware DNA
