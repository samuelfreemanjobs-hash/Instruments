# DiskLordz Sonic Constitution

**Permanent taste law.** All sounds and products are scored against this before approval.

## Core character (seek)

- aggressive  
- dirty  
- digital  
- mechanical  
- underground  
- heavy  
- punchy  
- imperfect  
- hypnotic  

## Textures (seek)

- digital aliasing  
- bit reduction  
- crushed transients  
- hardware *character* (classes, not clones)  
- sampler grit  
- degraded resampling  
- saturation  
- unusual pitch artifacts (when intentional)  

## Avoid

- generic EDM polish  
- sterile, “stock library” neutrality  
- excessive hi-fi cleanliness  
- generic trap-pack aesthetics  
- over-limited, loudness-war mush (unless product spec says otherwise)  

## Sonic scoring

Use `sonic-scoring.md` — **minimum 70/100** on constitution fit for `approved: true` on assets; **minimum 75** for flagship products.

Human Creative Director can override with documented reason in Airtable.

## Never ship

- Assets that fail constitution **and** fail provenance  
- Products positioned against constitution without explicit “experimental” label in genome
