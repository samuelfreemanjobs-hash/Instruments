# Business model

## Ladder (catalog first; no subscription at launch)

| Tier | Typical price | Example |
|------|---------------|---------|
| Free | $0 | Teaser pack, demo presets |
| Mini | $9–19 | Small expansion |
| Kit | $29–49 | Full drum library |
| Plugin | $49–99 | Effect or instrument |
| Bundle | $99–199 | Plugin + expansion |
| Collection | $199–299 | Family bundle |
| All-access | Later | After catalog depth |

## Revenue mix (target)

- Sample kits + expansions (volume)  
- Plugins + instruments (margin, brand)  
- Bundles (LTV)  
- Free tier → email → paid conversion  

## Unit economics (track in Airtable / analytics)

Per product: dev/agent cost, asset reuse %, support burden, refund rate, derivative SKUs possible.

See `../intelligence/ECONOMICS.md` (template).

## Licensing posture

- **Samples:** royalty-free personal/commercial per product genome unless stated  
- **Plugins:** simple license v1; no heavy DRM initially  
- **Stack:** VST3 SDK ≥3.8 (MIT); JUCE tier per `../docs/LICENSING.md`
