# Operating principles

1. **Factory over heroics** — build systems that compile products.  
2. **Artifacts over chat** — genomes, DNA, manifests, contracts.  
3. **Gates over trust** — automated provenance, QA, sonic score.  
4. **Teams over agent sprawl** — three meta-agents + role contracts.  
5. **Learn in public (internal)** — `knowledge/` for wins, failures, decisions.  
6. **Human at the edge** — approval for irreversible commercial actions.  
7. **Economics aware** — no autonomous burn without tracking (see ECONOMICS).  
8. **TRON separation** — reusable OS vs DiskLordz vertical IP.
