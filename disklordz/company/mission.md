# Mission

**DiskLordz builds recognizable underground digital audio tools and libraries for producers who want aggression, character, and imperfection—not sterile stock sounds.**

We operate as a **product factory**: market intelligence and creative direction become validated plugins, kits, presets, and expansions through a shared production engine.

## Success

- Catalog depth with **one sonic universe** (families, not random SKUs)  
- **Asset reuse** across kits, MPC, plugins, and content  
- **Defensible IP**: original DNA, Character Lab DSP, kernel products  
- Creative Director time on **taste and approval**, not file wrangling  

## Out of scope (mission level)

- Generic “500 trap drums” commodity packs with no point of view  
- Copying trademarked hardware branding or unauthorized sample sources  
- Subscription-first business before catalog proof
