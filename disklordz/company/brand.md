# Brand

## Name

**DiskLordz** — digital, heavy, underground, machine-forward.

## Voice

- Direct, confident, technical when needed  
- No hype-bro EDM marketing  
- Speak to **workflow and sonic problems**, not genre clichés  

## Visual (direction)

- Dark UI, high contrast, industrial / digital texture  
- Avoid generic neon trap-pack aesthetics  
- Product art: abstract degradation, waveforms, hardware-*inspired* (not counterfeit logos)  

## What we say

- “Sounds like a battered sampler, not a stock library.”  
- “Built for aggressive, imperfect, hypnotic drums.”  

## What we avoid

- “Industry-standard quality” / “radio ready” as primary claims  
- Implying endorsement by hardware brands
