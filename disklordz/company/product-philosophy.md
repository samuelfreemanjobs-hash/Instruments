# Product philosophy

## Sonic problems, not genre labels

Products are defined by **problems**:

- DIRTY DIGITAL KICKS  
- DARK 808 ARCHITECTURE  
- 2000s DIGITAL DRUMS  
- CRUSHED PERCUSSION  
- MEMPHIS-STYLE DRUM TEXTURES  
- LO-FI SAMPLER DRUM LAB  

Not: “Trap Drum Kit Vol. 47.”

## One R&D → product family

One sonic concept compiles to WAV, MPC, Maschine, plugin, presets, teaser, bundle, content — via **Product Genome** + **Compiler**.

## Reuse first

Before new synthesis: query asset graph for unused or underused DNA that fits the genome.

## Good product (checklist)

- [ ] Genome complete  
- [ ] Constitution score meets threshold  
- [ ] Provenance 100%  
- [ ] QA gates passed  
- [ ] Demos prove the **problem** solved  
- [ ] Ladder tier intentional  
- [ ] Family link set  

## Bad product (reject)

- Vague positioning  
- Duplicate SKU without differentiation  
- Assets without hash + DNA  
- Plugin outside Plugin Kernel  
- **AI slop** — see [.cursor/rules/no-ai-slop.mdc](../../.cursor/rules/no-ai-slop.mdc) (generic copy, unvalidated AI output, volume without gates)
